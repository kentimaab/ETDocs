### Axis probe

Ethiris supports probing against certain camera brands. Probing is currently supported for Axis cameras, which means that all cameras with firmware **4.40** or later can be connected to Ethiris.

Ethiris supports video, PTZ, I/O, audio, and events via probed Axis cameras.

### ONVIF

Ethiris supports video, PTZ, I/O, audio, and events via ONVIF.

Kentima has not tested every camera model on the market, but cameras that support **ONVIF Profile S** can likely be connected to Ethiris.

### Generic

Ethiris also supports video via RTSP. This means that cameras with RTSP support can be connected to Ethiris by selecting the **Generic** type and then specifying a URL for retrieving video from the camera.

!!! note
    This means that the vast majority of IP cameras and video encoders can likely be connected to Ethiris VMS via Axis probe, ONVIF, or Generic.
